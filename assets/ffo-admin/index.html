Welcome to Film Facilitation Office Application Portal!

Please go through our Step-by-Step guide carefully before filling out the application form. Your application must be accompanied with a payment of INR equivalent to USD 225, and will not normally take more than three weeks to process. Payment once made is not refundable.

Guidelines

  • Filming at Airports / Aerial Filming permission from Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA) - Kindly apply at least two (2) months prior to the day of the shoot.
  • Filming at Border Areas - Kindly apply at least two (2) months prior to the day of the shoot.
  • Filming at Railways - Kindly apply at least two (2) months prior to the day of the shoot.
